Building kit: eight lots

One procedural low-poly kit, drawn as isometric SVG with the world's ink outline. Every lot is a few boxes, prisms and frusta (the same primitives the 3D kit will merge per district), one signature prop marked with a brass dot, and a ground plate tinted with its district accent. Shape counts under each card (faces, plus rods and ropes counted once) are measured from the drawing.

Two style options, same geometry. Style 1 · Day, flat ink is what the palette texture and hull outline render at noon. Style 2 · Dusk, lit uses the second palette texture: cooler walls, emissive windows and a lantern pool on the ground. It previews the night work and costs no extra draws (a texture swap).

District accents are suggested, one per lot, so all eight districts appear. Final lot assignment is the build task's call; the Brig is the real space “The Brig” (index 14). Fonts fall back to Georgia and Consolas here (nothing loads from the network).

Recommendation: Style 1 as the default look, with Style 2 as the night mood. Style 1 keeps every prop readable at street zoom; Style 2 is the same models under the night texture, so adopting both costs nothing.
