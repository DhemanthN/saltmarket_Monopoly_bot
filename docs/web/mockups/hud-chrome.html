Three options. Each is shown at phone (390×844) and desktop (1440×900), with the Menu open in a second row. Demo values only: turn 14, round 4, dusk, cash 1,240. The lot is a real board space (Honeyglass Strand, rents from data/board.json). No wind or other invented telemetry.

Rules shown: brackets and compass never overlap the decision panel or the Menu button (the badge under each frame is measured live by script). Instruments (compass, telemetry, Menu button) are night-panel signboards; documents (ledger, deed, Menu) are paper. Brass over the dark world uses a new ornament-only token brass-lit #C9A24A, 3.6:1 against the dusk sea. Fonts fall back to Georgia and Consolas here because nothing loads from the network; the build self-hosts Cinzel (headings) and JetBrains Mono (figures).

Recommendation: A. It keeps the compass where players expect it (top centre), puts the ledger on the right where it never meets the deed, and spends the least screen on phone. B has the most character but its dial, left rail and right-docked deed crowd a laptop. C has the best vignette and a true 0–360 strip; borrow its vignette for A.
