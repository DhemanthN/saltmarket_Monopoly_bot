Loading screen: three options

Dark ocean blueprint. The dotted route is split into four legs, one per real loading stage: runtime download, Python start, models, first scene. The percent comes from bytes downloaded during stage 1 and from stage completion after that, so it never moves without a real event. The frames show an example state, stage 2 of 4. Use the buttons to step through stages.

Preview stage:

A. Full chart, route across the screen

Route winds from a harbour mark past three islets (one per stage) to the X. Percent and caption sit low in a quiet card. Most map-like.

B. Compass rose and route strip

A slowly turning compass rose (static under reduced motion) in the centre, route as a straight dotted strip under it with four waypoints. Least busy, best on phone.

C. Logbook entries beside the chart

Chart on one side, a ship's log listing each stage with its real elapsed time on the other (desktop); on phone the log stacks under the chart. Most informative; useful for slow connections.
