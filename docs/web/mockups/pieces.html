Seat pieces: four shapes

Four pieces, one per seat: sloop, skull flag, compass, anchor. A seat is readable three ways at once: shape (silhouette), owner colour (plinth ring, cloth, and the body in Option A) and emblem (ink emblem on a sailcloth disc, custom SVG, never emoji). Owner colours and emblems are the DESIGN.md ones: saffron sun, teal wave, vermilion star, indigo anchor.

Two options. A · Painted tokens: the whole piece takes the owner colour, so seats read from across the board. B · Carved and brass: bodies are wood, brass or iron; owner colour sits on sails, flag, needle, rope wrap and the plinth ring, as DESIGN.md says (owner colour on cloth, emblem on a flag). B looks more like objects from the island; A is stronger on colour alone.

Pairing note: seat 4's anchor piece also carries the anchor emblem on purpose, so that seat reads two ways. Game-scale sizes below (44 px street view, 36 px phone, 24 px chart view) are my assumption to tune in 3D, not measured. Fonts fall back to Georgia and Consolas here (nothing loads from the network).

Recommendation: B. It keeps the world's paper-and-brass look, and the silhouette test (black row) shows all four shapes stay distinct without colour. Take A's stronger colour mass for the plinth ring (already in B) and the ledger chips.
